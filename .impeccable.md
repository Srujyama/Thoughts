## Design Context

### Users

This is a private, personal notes and knowledge-management app for its owner. It is used on both desktop and mobile to capture thoughts, maintain lists, organize notes into folders, and edit Markdown without friction.

### Brand Personality

Warm, minimal, and editorial. The interface should feel like a dependable personal writing tool with a quiet typewriter character: focused and tactile, never corporate or flashy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the existing cream-and-brown typewriter theme shown in the supplied references, including its restrained borders, monospaced editorial typography, and low-distraction workspace. Desktop can be information-dense; mobile should adapt controls for touch while retaining all core writing actions. Avoid generic dashboard cards, neon effects, glassmorphism, and ornamental gradients.

### Design Principles

1. Put note content and reliable saving ahead of decoration.
2. Preserve a calm, paper-like writing surface and strong typographic hierarchy.
3. Keep common editing and navigation actions immediately reachable on desktop and mobile.
4. Adapt layouts to the viewport instead of merely shrinking the desktop interface.
5. Make loading, offline, and error states accurate, recoverable, and non-destructive.
